My favorite movie


Cars 2


Yes, I know Cars 2 is considered the weakest movie of the Cars franchise, but I like it the best. I like the fact it's more of a spy movie, and you can meet so many different cars in addition to having the main cast there. I like Mater's subplot because it kind of enhances his character and the way he feels is relatable to me.

My favorite character is Holly Shiftwell because she can fly and she's just cool. I used to have plastic figures of the cars from Cars 2 and hers was my favorite out of all of them

There is this Weezer song in the movie that I memorized the whole chorus to because of how good it was. That was my first intro to Weezer. I also reference the movie quite a bit, like calling wasabi "pistacio ice cream"

Most people won't agree with me, but I think it's the best movie in the whole franchise.
