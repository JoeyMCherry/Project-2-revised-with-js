Favorite place I ever traveled to


CANADA!!!


Specifically, Toronto, Ontario. We drove there and back. We also drove from Toronto to Niagara falls (Canadian side). While we were in Canada, we saw a couple hockey games from outside Scotiabank Arena (Where the Maple Leafs play)

In addition to that, we went to many restaurants, bought lots of clothes, walked around quite a bit, and took a tour in a castle! The fall leaves were really pretty! I still have some leaves that I collected from there.

We drove around a lot. Because of that, we found out that the traffic there is a bit insane. The city was suprisingly clean, which we enjoyed. We felt safe there. Because of my experience there, I decided I'd like to live there in the future for college.

I went up the CN tower which was very fun. I took a lot of photos (as the photographer that I am, naturally!)
