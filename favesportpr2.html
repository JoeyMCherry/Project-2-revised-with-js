Favorite sport and hobbies


Favorite sport: hockey


I've been playing hockey for 3 seasons (this year will be my 4th). I am a forward wing (almost never center) and I played goalie once in my second season when the goalie, Nick Jennings, got sick, and I volunteered to take his place during the game. So I suited up in his pads and eneded up winning 10-4.

Also on my second season, we won the championship! We won the Guenin cup and have our victors banner in the Carmel ice rink. For some reason they have not put it up in the Fuel Tank because they say Coach Rues is a Carmel coach (despite having her other banner in Fishers!)

I have a few game pucks from my years of playing. In fact, my best game puck was during the second season (yet again!) when we were tied during the championship. I got a goal and broke the tie. That then motivated my teammates to score just seconds after I did, winning us the game and letting us move on deeper into the championship. One of my teammates told me that we wouldn't have won the tournament without my goal.

Some things I have learned from my years of hockey:

  • The best hockey players have long hair and let it flow. Don't tie it back, just tuck it behind your ears
  • Never say shutout before the game ends
  • Hockey players are real superstitious sometimes. Don't question it.
  • Skate like your life depends on it. Quick shifts are best
  • Kneel for anyone who gets hurt until they get back up. I don't care that they're not on your team. Kneel for them. It's the right thing to do
  • Bring water or drink from your teammates. No water is miserable
  • Sometimes you just got to get a penalty because it could have been a goal against you
